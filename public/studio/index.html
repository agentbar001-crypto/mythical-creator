What gets sent

Only when you press an AI button, the active character's record (name, place, memory, tags, and lyrics) is sent to Anthropic's Claude to write that piece. It isn't stored by this app. In offline mode nothing leaves your device. See the privacy policy.

Your Own Anthropic Key (Optional)

Used after your free samples run out, so you pay Anthropic directly for your own use. Get a key at console.anthropic.com. Your key goes only to this app's server to make your request, and is never stored there.
